# Datomic at Scale — before the class

**2 hours, slides throughout.** Nothing to install, and nothing to run.

## What it covers

How a Datomic system performs in production, in four topics: **reads,
writes, parallelism and caches**. Each topic follows the same shape —
the basics of how it works, one easy example, and the main catches you
will actually hit in production.

It continues from *Datomic in Production*, but the only assumed
knowledge is the vocabulary — what a peer, a transactor and storage
are — and the class re-draws that map in its opening slides anyway.

## How the material is built

The goal is the right mental model, not a tuning reference. Settings
tables, benchmarks and deployment runbooks are deliberately left out;
they live in the Production class materials. Each mechanism is drawn
simply, and examples use small code and small numbers chosen to be
readable rather than impressive.

## Optional: reproducing the failover yourself

Part II shows a transactor failover (writes pause briefly, reads never
notice). `infra/HA.md` and `infra/pg-transactor-standby.properties`
have the two-transactor setup if you want to run it yourself: Docker
Postgres, a Datomic Pro distribution unzipped locally, and two
transactors pointed at an identical `sql-url`. Allow twenty minutes.

None of this is needed to attend.

## What to bring

A question about a system you actually operate. The catches sections
are more useful with a real workload to hold them against.
